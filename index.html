Privacy

Periapsis does not collect data. It does not track you. Nothing is linked to your identity, and nothing is used for tracking or advertising.

The game is offline. It does not send gameplay, diagnostics, or a device identifier anywhere. There is no network call in normal play, no analytics, and no account to sign in to.

Your progress is a local save on the device you are playing on. It stays there. Uninstalling the app, or clearing the app’s storage, deletes it. There is no cloud copy.

Age rating expected for the store listing: 9+. Ships explode. There is no blood, gore, profanity, gambling, or user-generated content.

Support

Email support@clarkestudios.com. Say which device you are on and what you expected to happen.

Where is my save?

On the device, in the app’s local storage, under the key periapsis-v2. It is not on a server. A new phone does not have the old save.

How do I reset?

Delete Periapsis and install it again, or clear the app’s stored data in system settings. That drops the local save, including stars and the hangar. There is no account to delete.

Is the game offline?

Yes. After it is installed, play does not need a connection. Airplane mode is a normal way to play.
